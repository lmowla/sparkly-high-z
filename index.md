**Sparkly** is a monthly Zoom series on observations and simulations of star clusters in galaxies across redshift.

With the help of strong gravitational lensing, JWST now resolves individual star clusters and proto-globular cluster candidates from z ~ 1 to beyond z ~ 9. Sparkly connects these observations to studies of young and globular clusters in the local Universe, to galaxy scaling relations, and to simulations and theories of cluster formation and evolution. Together, we aim to understand how star clusters form, how and which ones survive to the present day, and what they reveal about the assembly of their host galaxies and dark matter haloes, including their contribution to reionization and their role as possible hosts of intermediate-mass black holes.
