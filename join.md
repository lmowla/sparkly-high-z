Sparkly is open to anyone working on star clusters, from local globular clusters to the high-redshift Universe, and to anyone who wants to learn about them.

## Mailing list

Announcements of upcoming talks are sent by email. To be added to the list, write to **Lamiya Mowla** (lmowla@wellesley.edu) with your name and institution.

## Zoom

The Zoom link is sent with each meeting announcement. It is not posted here to avoid uninvited guests.

## Speaking

We welcome suggestions for speakers and topics, including your own work. Suggest a talk by writing to any of the [organizers]({{ '/organizers/' | relative_url }}).

## Editing this site

Organizers and members with access to the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/lmowla/sparkly-high-z) can edit any page directly on GitHub; see the README there for instructions.
